Master Supply Agreement

between Brandywine Heritage Farms & Fisheries, Inc. ("Supplier") and Bleu Hen Hospitality Group, LLC ("Buyer")
Effective August 1, 2024 · Initial Term through July 31, 2027

§ 2. Exclusivity; Minimum Purchases

2.1 During the Term, Buyer shall purchase all of its requirements for poultry, pork, beef and finfish exclusively from Supplier.

2.2 Buyer shall purchase Products having an aggregate invoiced value of not less than One Million One Hundred Thousand Dollars ($1,100,000) in each Contract Year (the "Annual Minimum").

§ 5. Origin and Labeling Warranty

5.1 Supplier represents and warrants that each Product delivered hereunder (a) conforms to the description, origin and labeling on its packaging and invoice, and (b) was raised, grown or caught within seventy-five (75) miles of Buyer's principal place of business (the "Origin Standard").

5.2 Supplier shall not substitute product from any other source without Buyer's prior written consent.

§ 9. Inspection, Verification and Audit

9.4 Verification Hold. If Buyer has reasonable grounds to question the origin or labeling of any lot of Product, Buyer may, upon written notice to Supplier, place such lot on verification hold for a period not to exceed seventy-two (72) hours. During a verification hold, (a) Buyer shall not be in breach of Section 2, (b) Buyer shall have no obligation to pay liquidated damages under Section 12, and (c) Supplier shall deliver chain-of-custody documentation for such lot within forty-eight (48) hours of such notice.

9.5 Audit. Buyer may audit Supplier's records upon five (5) business days' notice or, where Buyer has reasonable grounds to suspect a breach of Section 5, upon twenty-four (24) hours' notice.

§ 11. Termination

11.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for Material Breach not cured within thirty (30) days after written notice.

11.2 Notwithstanding Section 11.1, Buyer may terminate immediately upon written notice if Supplier willfully mislabels or substitutes Product in breach of Section 5.

§ 12. Liquidated Damages

If Buyer terminates this Agreement other than pursuant to Section 11, Buyer shall pay Supplier, as liquidated damages and not as a penalty, an amount equal to twenty-five percent (25%) of the Annual Minimum remaining for the unexpired portion of the Term.

§ 14. Non-Disparagement

Neither party shall make any public statement concerning the other that is materially false or misleading. Nothing in this Section restricts (a) truthful statements, (b) statements required by law, or (c) statements made in good faith to protect the health, safety or reasonable expectations of customers.

§ 15. Indemnification

Supplier shall indemnify Buyer for direct losses arising from a breach of Section 5, including the cost of replacement Product, customer refunds and reasonable attorneys' fees. Neither party shall be liable to the other for lost profits or reputational harm.
