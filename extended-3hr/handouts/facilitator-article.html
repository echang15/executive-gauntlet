Raised Close, Packed Far: A Beloved Delaware Supplier Is Reselling Industrial Meat and Seafood as "Local"
By Jo Alvarez · The Delmarva Dispatch · 5:00 PM

For at least 14 months, Brandywine Heritage Farms & Fisheries, the Sussex County supplier whose name appears on the menus of Bleu Hen Hospitality Group, has been repackaging poultry and pork from large out-of-state processors and selling it as "pasture-raised" and "local," according to a former Brandywine plant manager, two current employees and shipping records reviewed by the Dispatch.

The former plant manager estimates roughly half of Brandywine's volume now comes from outside the region. Plant codes found under Brandywine labels at Bleu Hen's Riverfront kitchen trace to facilities in North Carolina and Pennsylvania.

Brandywine founder Calvin Teague called the claims "false and malicious." Pressed on the plant codes Thursday, he acknowledged "limited co-packing at peak harvest" but said "everything is still from our farms."
