The Delmarva Dispatch · Food

The Hen Who Fed Delaware

By Jo Alvarez · March 2024

Ten years ago, Dana started with a 40-seat bistro on the Wilmington riverfront and a hand-painted sign: a blue hen holding a fork. Today, Bleu Hen Hospitality Group runs three restaurants, employs 140 people and has won the state's "Best Farm-to-Table" award three years running.

The secret, Dana says, is not the food. It's the promise on the menu: raised, grown or caught within seventy-five miles of your table.

"I walk every farm we buy from, twice a year. If I haven't looked a farmer in the eye, it doesn't go on my menu."

Among those farms is Brandywine Heritage Farms & Fisheries in Sussex County, where founder Calvin Teague says he has raised animals "slowly and honestly" for two decades. Dana and Teague signed an exclusive three-year supply agreement this summer.

"Cal is the real thing," Dana says. "He's the reason I can say what we say."

The next goal is a fourth location. Dana won't say where. "When I can look the farmer in the eye," they say, laughing.
