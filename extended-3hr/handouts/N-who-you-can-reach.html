Call rules

  • Call tokens: 12 per team of 10 (8 per team of 5). Each call costs 1 token. The field check costs 2 and takes 8 minutes.
  • Call Slip: Write who you're calling and why. One caller at a time per team. The call lasts 3 minutes.
  • Inbound calls and visits are free and come without warning. Expect to answer within 90 seconds.
  • Orders are binding. If you tell someone to do something, it's done.
  • Everything you say can be used. Including to the reporter.
  • No eavesdropping on the other team's calls.
