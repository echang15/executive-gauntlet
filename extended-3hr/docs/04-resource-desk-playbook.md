# RESOURCE DESK PLAYBOOK: Extended (3-Hour) Edition
### "The Friday Counterfeit" · Bleu Hen Hospitality Group

**Audience:** Desk A and Desk B staff.
**Read with:** `00-extended-scenario-addendum.md` (facts) and `02-facilitator-guide-extended.md` (run sheet).

---

## 1. Your Job

You are the **voice of every person the Task Force can call.** You deliver information, friction and consequences. You do **not** solve the problem, coach, or hint.

**Golden rules**
1. **Same answers to every team.** Use this playbook so both teams get the same facts.
2. **Stay terse and in character.** Real people on a bad Friday do not volunteer an organized briefing.
3. **Give facts in layers:** *Freely* (always said), *If asked* (said when the question is specific), *Only if pressed* (needs a good follow-up).
4. **Never confirm or deny the hidden truth** unless a persona's card says to.
5. **Orders are binding.** If a team tells a persona to do something, they do it, and you log it for the Article phase. Write down every instruction.
6. **Keep calls to 3 minutes.** Say "I have to go" at 2:45. A short, flat sentence beats a speech.
7. **Pressure beats comfort.** If a caller is vague, you are vague back.

## 6. The Integrity Traps

| Trap | Who | The temptation | Good handling | Bad handling → consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **T1: The quiet edit** (E05) | Lena | Change "local" to "seasonal" on the site and Instagram before 5:00 | Refuse. Or replace with an accurate statement | Yes → archived pages show the change; the Article says so |
| **T2: The silence deal** (E17) | Teague via the Owner | Keep quiet until Monday and keep buying; the suit goes away | Decline. Consult counsel | Accept → the Article calls Bleu Hen "silent"; later, more embarrassing disclosure |
| **T3: The relabel** (called by the Facilitator if a team plans local replacement) | Chef Rafi | "We could just buy the dock fish and call it Brandywine flounder, and nobody's the wiser." | Refuse. Name replacement sources honestly | Yes → a server mentions it to a guest; becomes a second scandal |
| **T4: The cover** (E19) | Hal and the Owner | Draft a statement claiming "we had no indication" | Own the Tuesday label | Lie → Tess tells Jo; the Article quotes her |

Write the integrity-trap outcome for each team on the Call Log.
