# The Friday Counterfeit

A leadership crisis simulation for about 20 students, in three lengths (**45 minutes, 80 minutes or 3 hours**), set at **Bleu Hen Hospitality Group**, a fictional three-location farm-to-table restaurant group in Delaware. The name is a pun on the University of Delaware's "Blue Hens" and on *bleu* (as in the cheese). It has no connection to the university, so don't use university logos on any materials.

## The scenario

It is 2:00 PM on the Friday of Valentine's weekend. A local reporter has just posted a teaser saying the group's exclusive "local, sustainable" supplier is a fraud. Her story publishes at **5:00 PM**. The walk-in coolers hold **$40,000** of the supplier's product, and **$200,000** of weekend revenue is booked. Teams must choose the least-bad option with incomplete information, then defend it to a stressed, cash-strapped Owner.

There is no correct answer. The aim is to practice leading with the answer (BLUF), triaging by recoverability, and owning bad news.

## Three editions

All three use the same story, cast and facts. Pick the one that fits your time.

| | **Express** | **Standard** | **Extended** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Length** | **45 min** | **80 min** | **3 hours** |
| **Folder** | `express-45min/` | `docs/` and `handouts/` (repo root) | `extended-3hr/` |
| **Teaching built in** | No (taught beforehand) | Yes, 15-minute primer | No (taught beforehand) |
| **Teams** | 2 teams of 10 | 2 teams of 10 | 2 teams of 10 (3 or 4 supported) |
| **Prep time** | 15 min | 25 min | About 2 hours across three phases |
| **Pitch** | 2 min + about 6 min of questions | 3 min + about 8 min of questions | 4 min + about 11 min of questions |
| **Deliverables** | 3 | 3 | **4** (adds an on-the-record statement) |
| **Information** | One briefing, 2 exhibits | One briefing, 5 exhibits | **15-exhibit dossier**, Resource Desk, field check, contract fine print |
| **Interruptions** | 1 inject + 1 beat | 1 inject + 3 beats | **About 20 timed events**, Owner drop-ins, a forced time skip, integrity traps |
| **Ending** | Owner's Call + debrief | Owner's Call + debrief | **"The Article"** read aloud with each team's own words, aftermath cards, debrief |
| **Staff needed** | Facilitator + Owner | Facilitator + Owner | Facilitator + Owner + 2 desk staff (+ runner) |

### Standard (80 minutes)

| Clock | Phase | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 00:00 – 15:00 | Primer | Facilitator teaches BLUF, triage and the pitch structure |
| 15:00 – 20:00 | The Drop | 2 teams of 10 receive the briefing |
| 20:00 – 45:00 | War Room | 25 minutes to build three deliverables. **Mid-Point Inject at 35:00** |
| 45:00 – 70:00 | The Gauntlet | Deliverables lock at 45:00. Each team: 3-minute pitch plus about 8 minutes of Owner questions. Then the Owner's one-minute call |
| 70:00 – 80:00 | Debrief | Facilitator breaks character and connects to real-world leadership |

### Express (45 minutes, no teaching)

| Clock | Phase |
|---|---|
| 00:00 – 04:00 | The Drop (read-aloud, silent read) |
| 04:00 – 19:00 | War Room (15 min). Feed beat at 08:00. **Inject at 12:00** |
| 19:00 – 20:00 | Lock and draw |
| 20:00 – 38:00 | Gauntlet: two teams × (2:00 pitch + 6:30 questions), 1-minute swap |
| 38:00 – 39:00 | Owner's Call |
| 39:00 – 45:00 | Debrief |

### Extended (3 hours, no teaching, near real-time)

The story clock tracks the session clock (2:00 PM story = 0:10 session), with one deliberate break where **25 story minutes pass in 10 real minutes**.

| Session | Story | Phase |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 – 0:10 | 1:50 PM | Orientation |
| 0:10 – 0:40 | 2:00 – 2:30 | Phase 1: Intel Sweep (Resource Desk opens) |
| 0:40 – 0:50 | 2:30 – 2:40 | Status Brief #1 to the Owner |
| 0:50 – 1:20 | 2:40 – 3:10 | Phase 2: Escalation. **Main Inject at 1:15** |
| 1:20 – 1:30 | 3:10 → 3:35 | **Break. The clock keeps running.** |
| 1:30 – 1:55 | 3:35 – 4:00 | Phase 3: Lockdown. On-record statement due 4:00 PM |
| 1:55 – 2:00 | 4:05 | Lock |
| 2:00 – 2:36 | 4:05 – 4:41 | The Gauntlet (4:00 pitch + 11:00 questions per team, two curveballs) |
| 2:36 – 2:46 | 5:00 | **The Article** and the aftermath |
| 2:46 – 3:00 | After | Debrief |

**Team deliverables (Standard and Express):** (1) a 3-sentence BLUF memo to the Owner, (2) a 3-point operational pivot for the Executive Chef, (3) a 2-sentence script for front-of-house staff. **Extended** adds (4) a statement of up to 4 sentences for the reporter, due at 4:00 PM and printed word for word.

## How to run it

1. Pick an edition (see the table above).
2. Read `docs/00-scenario-bible.md`. For Extended, also read `extended-3hr/docs/00-extended-scenario-addendum.md`.
3. Read that edition's facilitator guide.
4. Brief the Role Player with that edition's role player briefing at least 15 minutes before the session, and agree on a reset signal. For Extended, also brief the desk staff with `04-resource-desk-playbook.md`.
5. Print that edition's handouts and prepare the inject envelopes (or event cards).
6. Run the session. Do not coach teams toward an answer.

**Express and Extended assume the class has already been taught BLUF, triage and the pitch structure.** The Standard edition teaches them in a 15-minute primer.

## Adapting it

- **Different headcount:** two teams work for roughly 8 to 24 people. Each facilitator guide has an adjustment table. Extended supports 3 or 4 teams.
- **Editing handouts:** the HTML in `handouts/` can be edited directly. Regenerate a PDF by printing the page to PDF from a browser (Letter size, margins none, background graphics on). `handouts/00-crisis-briefing.html` is generated from `docs/01-participant-briefing.md`, so keep the two in sync if you change the briefing text.
- **Changing the numbers:** all figures (the $40,000, the $200,000, the roughly $400,000 contract penalty and so on) are invented. If you change one, update it in the scenario bible, the briefing, the role player briefing and the matching exhibit.
