# FACILITATOR'S GUIDE: Express (45-Minute) Edition
### "The Friday Counterfeit" · Bleu Hen Hospitality Group

> **Read first:** `../../docs/00-scenario-bible.md` for names, numbers and the hidden truth.
> **Audience:** The facilitator. The Owner reads `03-role-player-express.md`.

**Format:** 45 minutes. **No teaching is built in.** Teams arrive already taught BLUF, triage and the pitch structure. If they weren't, hand each a copy of the Pitch Structure line on the worksheet and say they're on their own.
**Participants:** 20 students in 2 teams of 10 (adjustments in Section 10).
**Goal:** One compressed, high-stakes cycle: read, decide, draft, absorb a shock, defend. Pressure over polish.

---

## 1. Staffing and Setup

| Role | Who |
|---|---|
| **Facilitator** | You. Runs the clock, the Drop, the inject, the debrief |
| **The Owner** | Role Player |
| **Counsel** (optional) | A third person to play Hal Brandt for inject questions. If you have no third person, you answer as Counsel |

**Room**
- **Two team tables**, far apart, with 10 chairs each.
- **Visual countdown timer** visible to everyone.
- **Pitch stage:** a table and two chairs facing the room, for the Owner.
- **Holding area** (hallway) for the team pitching second. If none, the team sits silently at the back.

**Print list**
- 20 Express Briefings; 1 set of Exhibits A and C per team; 1 Express Worksheet per team
- **2 Mid-Point Inject cards** (Section 5), sealed in envelopes marked "OPEN AT 12:00"

**Pre-session checklist (arrive 20 minutes early)**
- [ ] Timer set to count **down** from 45:00 (start at the Drop).
- [ ] Owner briefed. Agree on a **reset signal**.
- [ ] Inject envelopes in your hand.
- [ ] Teams seated, briefings face down.

---

## 2. Master Timeline

| Clock | Phase | Min | Your action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **00:00 – 04:00** | **The Drop** | 4 | Read-aloud, then 2-minute silent read |
| **04:00 – 19:00** | **War Room** | 15 | Draft deliverables. **Inject at 12:00.** Feed beat at 8:00 |
| **19:00 – 20:00** | **Lock** | 1 | Collect worksheets. Draw order |
| **20:00 – 28:30** | **Team 1** | 8.5 | 2:00 pitch + 6:30 Owner questions |

| **29:30 – 38:00** | **Team 2** | 8.5 | 2:00 pitch + 6:30 Owner questions |
| **38:00 – 39:00** | **Owner's Call** | 1 | Owner picks, in character |
| **39:00 – 45:00** | **Debrief** | 6 | Section 7 |

## 3. The Drop (00:00 – 04:00)

Music off. Lights low if you can. Read slowly, with no commentary:

> *"It is 2:00 PM, Friday, February thirteenth. In the kitchen at the Riverfront Bistro, the flounder is portioned and the roulade is rolled. Eleven hundred and thirty guests are coming this weekend: first dates, anniversaries, a few marriage proposals. Nineteen minutes ago, a reporter put out a message that says your 'local, sustainable' supplier is a fraud, and that she has proof. She publishes at five. The doors open at five. Your Owner is waiting for you. You've been taught the tools. Today, nobody will teach you. Turn over your briefing. You have fifteen minutes."*

- **00:00 – 01:30:** Read-aloud.
- **01:30 – 04:00:** Silent read. **Start the 15:00 War Room timer at 04:00 sharp.** Answer **logistics** questions only. Strategy questions get: "That's one of the things you have to decide."

---

## 4. The War Room (04:00 – 19:00)

Walk the room. **Do not coach.** Note who leads, who's silent, how the team splits and what it decides.

**Time calls**
- **08:00:** Deliver **The Feed** (below).
- **12:00:** **Mid-Point Inject.**
- **14:00:** "Five minutes. Reconvene and check your three deliverables match."
- **17:00:** "Two minutes. Spokespeople, stand up."
- **18:00:** "One minute."
- **19:00:** "Pens down. Hand me your worksheets."

### 08:00: The Feed (read aloud to the whole room, 15 seconds)
> *"Update from Marketing. The reporter's post is past six thousand shares. Two TV stations have called. Reservation system: thirty-one cancellations. Eleven servers at the Bistro have asked what to say to guests."*

### 12:00: Mid-Point Inject
At **11:30**, stand in the center holding both envelopes.
> *[Lower your voice. Walk quickly.]* "Stop. New information. Hands off your pens for ten seconds."
> *[Hand an envelope to each table.]* "This was just delivered. Read it as a team. The clock does not stop."

- Don't announce the contents. Don't extend time.
- Give the envelope to the **spokesperson,** not the loudest voice.
- Watch the first 60 seconds. It is your best debrief material.

**Inject card text** (print on red or yellow paper)

> **URGENT: 3:05 PM**
> **From: Hal Brandt, Outside Counsel**
>
> Calvin Teague has responded. His public statement: *"These claims are false and malicious. Brandywine has fed Delaware families for 22 years. We will not be bullied."* He is at a local TV station now.
>
> His attorney's letter to us says: if we **pull, return or publicly disparage** Brandywine product, he will file a **defamation suit** and pursue **breach-of-contract damages** under our exclusive supply agreement (18 months remain). No figure named.
>
> **My read:** I can't tell you whether the reporter is right. You have no proof either way. If you say anything public that sounds like an accusation, you may hand him his lawsuit. If you say nothing and it's true, you are serving guests something you promised you weren't.
>
> *The article still publishes at 5:00 PM. Lineup is at 4:15. Decide.*

### If teams ask questions about the inject
Answer as Counsel (Hal Brandt), tersely, consistent with the base canon:
- *"Can we get proof?"* "Not in three hours."
- *"How big are the penalties?"* "There's a damages clause. Low to mid six figures, plus fees. I'd want a day."
- *"Can we say we're 'pausing' the product?"* "You could. You'd have to defend that wording."
- *"Can we say we're investigating?"* "Then you'd better be."
- *"Can we call the reporter?"* "Lena can. Anything you say is on the record."
- *"Does insurance cover the $40K?"* "I doubt it."
- *"What does the Chef know?"* "Tess flagged a label to Rafi on Tuesday."

Do not resolve the dilemma for them.

---

## 5. The Gauntlet (20:00 – 38:00)

### 19:00: Lock and draw
Collect one copy of each team's worksheet (no edits after this). Draw pitch order by coin flip. **Team 2 leaves** for the hallway (or sits silent at the back). Tell them they may rehearse but not change anything.

### Each team (8.5 minutes)
1. Announce: **"Team [X], two minutes. Owner, the floor is yours."**
2. **Chime at 1:30 and 2:00.** The pitch ends at 2:00 even if the Owner has interrupted.
3. The Owner questions for about **6:30,** cold-calling at least **two** non-spokespeople.
4. **Hard stop** at 8:30. The Owner finishes the sentence.
5. Say: "Thank you. Next."

### Observation checklist (private)
- [ ] BLUF in the first sentence?
- [ ] Said the dollar cost?
- [ ] Addressed the supplier lawsuit threat?
- [ ] Clear ask?
- [ ] Deliverables consistent?
- [ ] Held eye contact and didn't read?
- [ ] Teammates besides the spokespeople could answer?
- [ ] Guest script sounded human?

### 38:00: The Owner's Call (1 minute)
After both pitches the Owner, in character, says which plan (or which parts) they'd act on and why in bottom-line terms, plus the one thing each team failed to answer. Not a grade.

---

## 6. If Time Runs Short

| Problem | Response |
|---|---|
| Teams haven't started by 08:00 | Walk over: "What's your decision?" |
| A team has no pitch order plan | You draw. Don't negotiate |
| Pitches run long | Chime at 2:00. Hard stop at 8:30. Protect the Owner's Call and the debrief |
| An inject question is hard | "Hal says: next question." |
| A team finishes early | "Say your guest script to a stranger. Does it hold up?" |

---

## 7. Debrief (39:00 – 45:00)

**39:00: Break character.** "That was a simulation. The stress was real."

**Question 1 (2 minutes):** *"You never knew whether the reporter was right. How did you decide? What would have changed your mind?"* Connect to incidents with incomplete data.

**Question 2 (2 minutes):** *"At 12:00 everything changed. What did your team do in the first 60 seconds: adjust, defend or freeze?"* Connect to adapting when the plan breaks and vendor or legal risk.

**Question 3 (1.5 minutes):** *"Two teams, one crisis. Did you pick the same path? What separated the pitches: strategy, clarity or ownership?"*

**Close (30 seconds), one line each:**
1. Lead with the answer.
2. Money comes back more often than trust.
3. Own the news. The story will be told either way.

**Optional reveal (only if time):** About half of Brandywine's volume was repackaged. The Owner and Chef weren't in on it. The signs (Tess's label, the price drop, the pork) were on the table.

---

## 8. Scoring (optional, private)

Rate each team 0 to 3 on: **BLUF clarity · cost honesty · consistency of the three deliverables · handling the inject · answering cold calls.** Use it for the debrief, not for ranking.

---

## 9. What Express Cuts

The Primer, the Resource Desk, Exhibits B, D and E, the field check, call tokens, the on-record statement (Deliverable 4), Status Brief, Owner drop-ins and the Article. **If you want any of these, run the Extended edition.**

## 10. Adjustments

| Class size | Adjustment |
|---|---|
| 12 to 16 | 2 teams of 6 to 8. Keep timings |
| 8 | 2 teams of 4. Run Q&A at 5 minutes |
| 24 to 30 | 3 teams. War Room 15 min. Each team: 1:30 pitch + 4:30 questions. Debrief 3 min |
