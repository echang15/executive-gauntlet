Meet Your Farmer

Brandywine Heritage Farms & Fisheries

Calvin Teague and his family have raised pasture-fed heritage animals in Sussex County, Delaware, since 2004. Their hens roam open fields. Their pigs are raised slow, on pasture, with no antibiotics. Their fishing partners bring in the flounder and rockfish on small boats, the same day, from Delaware Bay.

"My family has fed Delaware families for generations. Bleu Hen is the only restaurant group we trust to do it justice." Calvin Teague, Founder

Brandywine is our exclusive partner for poultry, pork, seafood and beef. When you eat at any Bleu Hen, you are eating from within seventy-five miles of this table. It's a promise we wrote in 2015, and one we keep.
