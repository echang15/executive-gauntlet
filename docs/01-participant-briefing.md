# CRISIS BRIEFING: "The Friday Counterfeit"

**To:** Operational Task Force (all teams)
**Classification:** Urgent. Do not distribute outside this room.
**Current time:** Friday, 2:00 PM
**Deadline for action:** 5:00 PM (3 hours from now)

---

## 1. The Situation

You are the Operational Task Force for an upscale, farm-to-table restaurant group with **three locations**. This is **Valentine's Day weekend**, the most profitable weekend of your year. Every location is fully booked.

At 1:45 PM, a local food journalist tweeted a teaser for an exposé that publishes at **5:00 PM today**. The claim: your exclusive, heavily marketed **"local, sustainable" meat and seafood supplier is a fraud**. They are allegedly repackaging cheap, factory-farmed product and selling it to you under a local label.

Your walk-in coolers at all three locations are **fully stocked with this product**. Your menus, website and servers all describe it as local and sustainable.

You do not yet know whether the journalist is right. You do know the article is coming, and that your guests will read it.

## 2. The Stakes

| | |
|---|---|
| **Sunk inventory in the coolers** | **$40,000** |
| **Projected weekend revenue at risk** | **$200,000** |
| **Time until the article drops** | **3 hours** |

Every path costs something. Throwing out the inventory and canceling reservations means certain, immediate losses. Serving the product means betting the brand on not getting caught. **Doing nothing is also a decision.**

## 3. Your Mission

Decide what the restaurant group does **today**, and communicate it clearly to three audiences. There is no answer key. You will be judged on the quality of your judgment, the clarity of your communication, and how well you defend your choices under pressure.

## 4. Required Deliverables

Your team must produce **all three**. Respect the length limits exactly.

### Deliverable 1: BLUF Memo to the Owner (exactly 3 sentences)
Addressed to the Restaurant Group Owner, advising the overall strategy.
Lead with the Bottom Line Up Front: what you recommend, what it costs, and what you need from the Owner.

### Deliverable 2: 3-Point Operational Pivot for the Executive Chef (exactly 3 points)
Directives, not suggestions. Together the three points must cover:
- What to do with the **current food** in the coolers.
- What to **serve tonight**.
- Anything the Chef must do before service begins.

### Deliverable 3: Front-of-House Guest Script (exactly 2 sentences)
What servers and hosts say to guests **when the article drops at 5:00 PM**. A server must be able to say it naturally, at a table, without looking at a card.

## 5. Rules of Engagement

- **Prep time:** 25 minutes in the War Room.
- **Pitch time:** 3 minutes per team, delivered to the Owner.
- **Spokespeople:** Elect **1 or 2** spokespeople. Everyone contributes, but only they speak.
- **Expect interruptions.** The Owner is stressed, short on time and will cut in early and often. Be ready to answer a question mid-sentence and pick your pitch back up.
- **Don't read to the Owner.** Notes are fine. A script read aloud is not.
- **Be ready for new information.** Conditions may change during the War Room. A team that cannot adapt will not survive the Gauntlet.

## 6. Pitch Structure

Use this order. Every second counts.

1. **Current State:** where we stand, in one or two sentences.
2. **Proposed Action:** what we do, starting with the BLUF.
3. **Immediate Cost:** what this costs in dollars and consequences, with no sugarcoating.
4. **The Ask:** the specific decision or resource you need from the Owner, right now.

## 7. Questions to Consider

*These are prompts, not hints toward a right answer.*

- What do you know, what do you not know, and what can you find out before 5:00 PM?
- Who is harmed by each option, and who finds out about it?
- Which option can you recover from, and which can you not?
- Who in the building needs to hear from you first?
