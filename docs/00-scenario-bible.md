# SCENARIO BIBLE: "The Friday Counterfeit"
### Bleu Hen Hospitality Group

**Audience:** Facilitator and Role Player ONLY. Do not hand to participants.
**Purpose:** One source of truth for names, numbers, backstory and "what is really going on," so every answer you improvise stays consistent across both teams.

> **Disclaimer:** All people, businesses and events here are fictional. The name is a pun on the University of Delaware's "Blue Hens" mascot and on *bleu* (as in the cheese). The group has no connection to the university. Do not use official university logos or branding on any materials.

## 1. The Brand

**Name:** **Bleu Hen Hospitality Group** (pun: *Blue Hen* + *bleu*, the French word for blue, as in *bleu cheese*).
**Tagline:** *"Raised Close. Served Honest."*
**Founded:** 2015 by the Owner, who started with one 40-seat bistro and a hand-painted sign of a blue hen with a fork.
**Reputation:** The most talked-about farm-to-table group in Delaware. Winner of "Best Farm-to-Table in the First State" three years running. Regular local TV segments. A wall of framed farm photos in every dining room ("Meet Your Farmers").
**The promise (printed on every menu, the website and the servers' lanyards):**
> *"Every bird, every cut, every catch: raised, grown or caught within 75 miles of your table."*

**Why it matters:** The "local, sustainable" promise is not a side feature. It is the reason guests pay about 40% more than at comparable restaurants. It is the brand.

## 3. The Supplier

**Brandywine Heritage Farms & Fisheries ("Brandywine")**
- **Founder / CEO:** Calvin Teague. Charismatic, a regular at farm festivals, beloved by local press.
- **Employs about 60 people** in Sussex County, many from families who have farmed there for generations.
- **Relationship:** Exclusive restaurant client is Bleu Hen. Teague has eaten at the Riverfront bistro and is on a first-name basis with the Chef.
- **Sustainability claims on the label:** "Pasture-raised," "Delaware Bay dayboat," "Family farm," "Antibiotic-free."
- **Reputation:** Passed a state agriculture inspection of the home farm last spring.

## 4. What Is Really Going On (FACILITATOR ONLY)

> **Do not reveal during play.** Keep it ambiguous. Use it only to keep answers consistent and, optionally, at the debrief.

**The truth, as written:**
- Roughly **half** of Brandywine's volume is repackaged from large out-of-state industrial processors and sold under the local label. The other half is genuinely local.
- It started about **14 months ago** when Brandywine lost a major wholesale contract and couldn't meet demand.
- The Chef's chicken and flounder are **mostly counterfeit**; the pork is **mixed**; the beef is **genuine**.
- The journalist has **a former Brandywine plant manager on record**, shipping records, and invoices. She does **not** have a comment from Teague that contradicts them.
- **The Owner and Chef were not part of it.** But there were warning signs they overlooked (see Section 6).

**Teams will never be able to confirm this by 5:00 PM.** The point of the exercise is deciding without proof.

## 6. The Evidence Trail (what the Task Force knows)

The briefing gives the Task Force these facts. Use this table if teams ask follow-up questions.

**Signals that the allegation may be true**
1. **Tuesday's delivery:** Tess peeled a shipping sticker and found a second label underneath with an **out-of-state plant code**. She photographed it. The photo is blurry. She sent it to Chef Rafi, who replied "pallets get re-boxed all the time."
2. **Price drop:** Brandywine's invoices fell **14% in November**; Teague called it "volume pricing."
3. **Quality drift:** Chef Rafi had noticed the "heritage" pork lacking its usual marbling for about three weeks.
4. **The journalist's tweet** says the supplier has a "paper trail," and names the supplier's restaurant client as a "three-location group."

**Signals that it may be false or overstated**
1. Two farm visits, most recently in September, looked genuine (staged or not, no one knows).
2. The state inspection passed last spring.
3. The teaser has no specifics, and one reading is that only part of the supply is affected.
4. Teague has years of goodwill and strong local support.

**Unknowns**
- Which products? All of them, or some?
- Since when?
- What does the journalist actually have?
- Did anyone at Brandywine tell Bleu Hen it was changing sources?
